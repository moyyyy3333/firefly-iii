# Claude Opus

> The most capable Claude tier — for the hardest reasoning and agentic work.

## Identity

- **Latest:** Opus 4.8
- **Model ID:** `claude-opus-4-8`
- **Family:** Claude 4.x
- **Tier:** Opus (frontier)

## Best for

- Deep multi-step reasoning and planning
- Complex agentic coding (drives [[Claude_Code]] well)
- Tasks where answer quality matters more than latency or cost

## Trade-offs

- Higher cost and latency than [[Claude_Sonnet]] / [[Claude_Haiku]]
- In [[Claude_Code]], a "fast mode" exists for Opus that speeds up output
  without downgrading to a smaller model.
